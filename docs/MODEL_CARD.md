# Model / System Card: Sentinel-AI

## Summary
- **Name:** Sentinel-AI Electoral Misinformation & Narrative Monitor
- **Version:** 0.2.0 (`2026-11-26`)
- **Authors:** Sentinel AI Labs — Victor Okpe, Jezreal Momoh, Joy Umofia
- **System Type:** Hybrid NLP + ONNX Semantic Embeddings (`fastembed` `all-MiniLM-L6-v2`, 384-dim) + `pgvector` Cosine RAG & Clustering + Optional Gemini 2.0 Flash Explainer/Vision Assistant.

## Intended use
- **Primary Users:** Accredited election observers, fact-checking newsrooms, CSOs, and INEC information desk officers.
- **Intended Tasks:** Claim triage (ranking risky rumors), semantic narrative clustering, retrieval of official INEC guidelines, guided media metadata screening, and drafting multilingual clarifications for human approval.
- **Out-of-Scope Uses:** Auto-declaring election results, determining voter eligibility, making legal findings of fraud, micro-targeting voters, or auto-publishing debunks without human review.

## Training and data
- **Knowledge Base:** 5 INEC Electoral Act 2022 / Regulations rules (`data/seeds/inec_verified_facts.json`) + 20 official multilingual voter FAQs (`data/official/voter_info_faq_multilingual.csv`).
- **Evaluation Benchmark:** 300 synthetic claims (`data/official/misinformation_claims_synthetic.jsonl`, 100% synthetic, zero personal data). See [DATASHEET.md](./DATASHEET.md).

## Evaluation
See [EVALUATION_REPORT.md](./EVALUATION_REPORT.md) for full per-language precision/recall/F1, cluster compression, abstention rate, and typo robustness metrics generated by `scripts/evaluate.py`.

## Error analysis
- **False Positives:** Urgent-sounding logistical reports (e.g., delayed materials) that also mention rigging-adjacent words can trigger medium/high risk; mitigated by mandatory human review.
- **False Negatives:** Novel slang or pure Hausa/Yoruba/Igbo phrasing outside lexical patterns can receive lower risk scores and lower English-embedding similarity.

## Fairness and bias
Tested across `en`, `pcm`, `ha`, `yo`, and `ig` samples. Diacritic stripping and regional electoral lexicons were added to reduce English-only bias; however, `all-MiniLM-L6-v2` remains English-dominant for deep semantic paraphrasing.

## Uncertainty and abstention
Confidence combines vector similarity (`0.65` weight) and risk signal (`0.35` weight). Whenever `confidence < 0.48` or no INEC rule exceeds `min_similarity = 0.25`, Sentinel-AI sets `should_abstain = True` and escalates for manual human verification.

## Human oversight
Every ingested claim defaults to `pending_human_review`. Accredited reviewers inspect rationale, INEC provenance, and draft replies in the Observer Console before recording an immutable `HumanReviewAudit` decision.

## Ethical and legal considerations
Strictly nonpartisan, NDPA 2023 compliant (automated PII redaction + author pseudonymization), never accesses live INEC systems, and includes explicit disclosures of third-party AI tools (FastEmbed ONNX, optional Gemini API).

## Maintenance
Issues and updates tracked at `github.com/Victorbjay/sentinel-ai`. Knowledge base facts can be updated idempotently via `scripts/seed_db.py`.
